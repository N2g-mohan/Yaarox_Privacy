1. Introduction

Welcome to Yaarox. Yaarox is a social media platform that allows users to create and share posts, short videos (Reels), communicate through private messaging, participate in community challenges and interact with other users.

This Privacy Policy explains how Yaarox collects, uses, stores, processes, protects and shares personal information when users access our mobile application or website. By using Yaarox, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account information

  • Username and email address.
  • Password, stored in hashed form rather than plain text.
  • Google account authentication identifiers and authentication-provider information.
  • Account and login status.

B. Profile information

  • Profile name, username, profile picture and biography.
  • Other information voluntarily added to a profile.
  • Information submitted with a verification request.

C. User-generated content

  • Posts, photographs, videos, Reels and captions.
  • Comments and other interactions.
  • Content visibility preferences, shared content and saved content.

D. Messages and attachments

  • Chat messages and images, videos or audio attachments shared through chat.
  • Message identifiers and conversation-related information.

E. Activity information

  • Likes, comments, shares, saves and follower relationships.
  • Post and Reel views, view duration and engagement analytics.
  • Notifications and challenge participation.

F. Technical and security information

We may process technical information such as IP addresses, authentication and session information, request information and administrative security logs where necessary for platform security, rate limiting, fraud prevention and system administration.

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Creating and managing user accounts.
  2. Providing login and authentication services, including Google Sign-In and account linking.
  3. Displaying profiles and uploaded content.
  4. Delivering posts and Reels according to visibility settings.
  5. Enabling private messaging and media sharing.
  6. Managing notifications and user interactions.
  7. Providing verification services and engagement analytics.
  8. Detecting spam, suspicious activity and unauthorized access.
  9. Reviewing reported content and enforcing Community Guidelines.
  10. Maintaining platform performance, reliability and security.
  11. Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Content Visibility and Privacy Controls

  • Public: Content may be viewed by users according to platform functionality.
  • Followers: Content is restricted to eligible followers or users included in the applicable Close Friends settings.
  • Private: Content is restricted according to the platform's private-content access controls.

Users are responsible for selecting appropriate visibility settings before publishing. Changing visibility settings may not remove copies that other users have already downloaded, captured or independently shared.

5. Google Sign-In and Authentication

Yaarox supports manual email/username authentication and Google Sign-In. When users authenticate through Google, we process verified authentication information required to establish or access their accounts. If a Google account matches an existing manually registered account, Yaarox may request explicit user confirmation before linking the accounts. We do not automatically link accounts without the required confirmation.

6. Chat and Private Communications

Messages and attachments are processed to deliver messages to intended participants, maintain conversation history, manage unread counts, prevent duplicate message processing, enforce participant access permissions, investigate reported abuse or comply with lawful requests.

Private messaging should not be assumed to have end-to-end encryption unless Yaarox explicitly introduces and verifies that functionality.

7. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Yaarox uses its own backend infrastructure, including Node.js, Express.js and MongoDB. Media may be stored using local server storage or supported cloud storage infrastructure. Third-party infrastructure and authentication providers may process relevant information when their services are enabled. These may include Google Authentication, Cloudinary when cloud storage is activated, Redis infrastructure when connected, and hosting or infrastructure providers.

We aim to ensure that third-party service providers process information only for authorized operational purposes and subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Data Security

Yaarox implements security measures designed to protect user information, including JWT-based authentication, bcrypt password hashing, role-based access control, request rate limiting, input validation and sanitization, protected media access, and administrative audit logging. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords.

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Yaarox does not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information may be disclosed in limited circumstances:

  1. To service providers supporting platform operations.
  2. When required by applicable laws or valid legal orders.
  3. To investigate security incidents, fraud or platform abuse.
  4. To protect users, platform integrity and legal rights.
  5. With user authorization or consent where applicable.

10. Data Retention

Yaarox retains information for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain platform security, resolve disputes and satisfy applicable legal requirements. When an account is deleted, associated information will be deleted or anonymized according to applicable retention requirements. Certain information may need to be retained for legally prescribed periods, including information required for investigations or regulatory compliance.

11. User Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may have the right to request access to their personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, raise privacy-related complaints and exercise other applicable data protection rights. Users may contact Yaarox through the official privacy contact below.

12. Children's Privacy

Yaarox recognizes the importance of protecting children's personal information. The platform will apply appropriate age-related safeguards and parental consent requirements wherever applicable under Indian law. Yaarox must not knowingly process children's personal data in violation of applicable legal requirements. The minimum permitted age and age-verification arrangements must be published in accordance with implemented safeguards and applicable law.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Yaarox may update this Privacy Policy when platform features, security practices or legal requirements change. Users will be informed of material changes through appropriate platform notifications or other communication methods.

Community Guidelines

Effective Date: [Insert Date]  ·  Last Updated: [Insert Date]
Our promise: These guidelines set expectations for respectful participation across Yaarox. Enforcement should follow a consistent, documented moderation process.

1. Our Community Mission

Welcome to the Yaarox community. Yaarox aims to create a safe, respectful and positive environment where people can express themselves, share creativity, communicate and build meaningful connections. Every user plays an important role in maintaining a healthy community.

These Guidelines apply to user profiles, posts and Reels, comments and interactions, private messages, media attachments, community challenges and other platform features.

2. Respect Everyone

Every user deserves respect. We do not allow bullying or targeted harassment, threats or intimidation, hate speech or discriminatory abuse, repeated unwanted contact, public humiliation or targeted attacks, or encouragement of violence against individuals or groups. Users should communicate respectfully, even when opinions differ.

3. No Harmful or Illegal Content

Users must not upload, publish, share or distribute content that violates applicable laws. Prohibited content includes child sexual exploitation or abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation or harassment, content promoting illegal activities, credible threats of violence, content exposing another person's private information without authorization, and material that facilitates fraud or criminal activity. Yaarox may remove such content and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

4. Protect User Privacy

Respect the privacy of others. Users must not publish another person's private contact details without authorization, share private conversations publicly without appropriate permission, distribute personal photographs or videos without the necessary rights, attempt to expose another user's identity or private information, or misuse information obtained through private messaging. Respect the privacy settings selected by other users.

5. Authenticity and Impersonation

Yaarox encourages authentic interactions. Users must not impersonate another person, create fake identities to deceive others, misrepresent official organizations, use verification badges misleadingly, create accounts for fraudulent or deceptive purposes, or manipulate other users through fabricated identities. Accounts found engaging in impersonation may be restricted or suspended.

6. Spam and Platform Manipulation

Users must not send repeated unwanted messages, publish repetitive promotional content, use fake accounts to manipulate engagement, artificially inflate likes, comments, views or followers, exploit platform bugs for unfair advantages, distribute malicious links or files, or use automated methods to abuse platform functionality.

7. Intellectual Property and Copyright

Users must respect the intellectual property rights of others. Do not upload or distribute copyrighted material without appropriate authorization, unauthorized photographs or videos, content that infringes trademarks, stolen creative work, or other protected material without the necessary rights. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to share content.

8. Safe and Responsible Challenges

Yaarox supports positive and creative community challenges. All challenges and related submissions must be safe and lawful. We do not permit challenges that encourage dangerous physical activities, promote illegal behavior, encourage harassment or humiliation, put participants or bystanders at risk, or encourage harmful behavior toward other people. Yaarox may remove unsafe challenges and restrict accounts involved in organizing or promoting them.

9. Responsible Communication

Users should maintain respectful behavior in public comments and private chats. Abusive or threatening messages, persistent unwanted communication, scams or fraudulent requests, harassment through repeated messages, sharing prohibited material, and attempts to obtain another user's account credentials are not permitted. Users may block or report accounts that make them uncomfortable.

10. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, report it through the available reporting functionality. Reports may relate to inappropriate posts or Reels, abusive comments, harassing messages, fake or impersonating accounts, privacy violations or other prohibited behavior. Please provide accurate information when submitting a report. False, malicious or deliberately misleading reports may themselves result in moderation action.

11. Moderation and Enforcement

Yaarox may review reported content and take appropriate moderation action. Depending on the severity and frequency of violations, actions may include:

  • Level 1 — Warning: Notification of a policy violation and request to stop or correct the behavior.
  • Level 2 — Content Restriction: Removal of violating posts, comments or media, or restriction of specific platform functionality.
  • Level 3 — Temporary Suspension: Temporary restriction of account access or features.
  • Level 4 — Permanent Account Suspension: Termination of accounts involved in serious or repeated violations.

Serious violations may result in immediate restrictions without prior warnings. Moderation decisions will consider the nature of the violation, available evidence, applicable law and user safety.

12. Appeals

Users who believe that moderation action was taken incorrectly may submit an appeal through the official support or grievance mechanism. Appeals should include relevant account information, details of the moderation decision and a clear explanation of why the decision should be reviewed. Yaarox may review appeals and communicate its decision through available support channels.

13. Our Commitment

  • Encouraging respectful interactions.
  • Protecting user privacy.
  • Maintaining a secure platform.
  • Addressing reported violations.
  • Supporting fair moderation.
  • Improving community safety.
  • Complying with applicable laws.
